# BODE Development

Welcome to **BODE** — a suite of powerful, accessible, and mostly free tools created to help individuals and businesses do more. 

From messaging and proxy testing to astrology and web utilities, our tools are open to everyone, with optional paid enhancements for even more capabilities.

Visit our website: [https://devs.uno](https://devs.uno)

---

## Available Tools

### [BODE Instant Messaging](https://devs.uno/im/)
A fast and global instant messaging platform that lets you chat with people worldwide, including texting real phone numbers. Free to use with enhanced capabilities available for premium users.

---

### [BODE Proxy](https://devs.uno/proxy-checker/)
Get access to fresh proxy lists and a powerful proxy checker tool to validate proxy functionality and anonymity levels.

---

### [SMTP Checker](https://devs.uno/smtp-checker/)
Quickly verify the health and configuration of any SMTP server. Useful for email marketers and developers managing outbound email systems.

---

### [Advanced Email Verifier](https://devs.uno/baev/)
A robust tool for verifying the validity of email addresses in bulk. Clean your mailing lists to reduce bounce rates and improve deliverability.

---

### [Email Extractor](https://devs.uno/baee/)
Extract email addresses from any text, web page, or uploaded file. Perfect for gathering leads or scraping contacts efficiently.

---

### [Inbox Mailer](https://devs.uno/bim/)
Send personalized email messages straight to inboxes. A lightweight, user-friendly mailer with built-in spam minimization and deliverability optimization.

---

### [Sitemap Generator](https://devs.uno/free-sitemap-generator/)
Automatically generate SEO-friendly sitemaps for your websites. Supports XML format, perfect for submitting to search engines.

---

### [Sitemap Indexer](https://devs.uno/free-sitemap-indexer/)
Submit and index your sitemaps quickly across major search engines to boost your site's visibility and crawl rate.

---

### [Robots.txt Generator](https://devs.uno/free-robots-txt-generator/)
Easily generate and customize `robots.txt` files to control how search engines crawl your website content.

---

### [BODE Oracle](https://bode.ng/consultation)
A digital Ifá divination experience that brings the wisdom of Yoruba tradition to your device. Ask life questions and receive spiritual insights.

---

### [BODE Wheel](https://bode.ng/wheel)
A beautifully crafted Zodiac Wheel that offers star prophecies, personality readings, and cosmic guidance based on your birth chart.
